Get started with UnReceipt.

Your accountant uses UnReceipt to collect receipts. Connect once, then send each receipt as it comes: your accountant sees it right away.

1

Open the invitation

In the email “<your accountant> wants to see your receipts”, press Yes, share. Or open the link or QR code your accountant gave you.

2

Create your account

Use the address the invitation was sent to. New to UnReceipt? Create account. No password? Sign in with an email code.

3

Say yes

The page asks “Share your receipts with <your accountant>?”. Press Yes, share. That's it: you're connected.

On iPhone (optional)

Get the UnReceipt app

On your iPhone, open testflight.apple.com/join/dE5HJCCz, install Apple's free TestFlight app when asked, then Install UnReceipt. Sign in with the same address. No iPhone? Everything works at app.unreceipt.com in any browser, on your phone too.

4

Send your receipts

Paper receipt: press the green +, then Take photo. UnReceipt reads the merchant, total, VAT and date; check them and save.

Email receipt: forward it to your own UnReceipt address, found in Settings → Send us receipts (Copy forwarding address). The guide there shows how to forward them automatically.

Many at once: Several at once (photos or a scan), up to 50.

5

Check what needs you

Receipts that arrive by email are read for you, then wait under Review needed. Open each one, compare it with the Original, then press Confirm receipt, or fill in what's in red and press Complete receipt.

On Mondays your accountant's email tells you what's still missing.
