Get started with UnReceipt.

Four steps from your first sign-in to your clients' receipts arriving on their own.

1

Sign in

Open app.unreceipt.com/app/login?next=/firm and sign in with the email address your firm was registered with. No password? Choose Sign in with an email code: you get a 6-digit code by email.

2

Join your firm

The page reads “<your firm> added you to its team”. Press Join <your firm>. You land on your client list.

3

Invite your clients

On the Clients page, press Add client: the company name, its org.nr (optional) and the email of the person who handles receipts. Press Send invite. To invite many at once, use Invite several (up to 25, one per line).

Or share your firm's link or QR code, at the bottom of the Clients page: a client opens it, signs in and presses Yes, share.

4

Watch the receipts arrive

Once a client says yes, they appear in your list. Their receipts come in as they photograph or forward them. Click a client to open their month.

ColumnMeans
NewArrived in the last 7 days.
Review neededNot checked yet, or the merchant, amount or date is missing.
ReadyChecked, not exported yet.
ExportedAlready in an export.

Next, when you're ready

  • Each month, on a client's page: import their bank statement (CSV) to see payments without a receipt, then Create export for a ZIP with a bookkeeping file, PDFs and the originals.
  • Colleagues: Settings → type their work email → Add. Tell them to sign in at app.unreceipt.com with that address.
  • Every Monday, clients get an email from your firm listing what's missing. You can switch it off per client.
