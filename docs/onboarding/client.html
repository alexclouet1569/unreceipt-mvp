Send your receipts

Three ways. Use the one that suits each receipt; most people use the first two.

Paper receipt

Take a photo

Right after you pay, before the receipt is lost or fades.

Email receipt

Forward it

To your own UnReceipt address. Set it up once and it can be automatic.

Lots of email receipts

Connect Gmail or Outlook

UnReceipt finds the receipts in your inbox by itself.

A · Take a photo

Press the green + button, then Take photo. Lay the receipt flat, in good light. UnReceipt reads the merchant, total, VAT and date; you check them, then press Save.

Also in that menu: Upload photo or PDF for a file you already have, and Several at once (photos or a scan) for up to 50 photos or a scanned stack. Photos, PDFs up to 10 pages, 4 MB each.

B · Forward email receipts

You have your own private address, in the form receipts+…@in.unreceipt.com. Find it in Settings → Send us receipts and press Copy forwarding address. Forward any receipt or invoice to it, attachments included.

For receipts that always come from the same senders (Uber, SJ, your phone bill…), the setup guide in Settings shows how to forward them automatically from Gmail, Outlook or iCloud.

C · Connect Gmail or Outlook

In Settings → Connected email, press Connect Gmail or Connect Outlook and approve on Google's or Microsoft's own page. UnReceipt only opens emails that look like receipts and ignores everything else. You can disconnect at any time.

Gmail shows a warning. During the pilot, Google displays “Google hasn't verified this app” while its review is in progress. If you'd rather not continue, forwarding (B) works just as well.

Check what needs you

A few minutes a week. Most receipts need nothing from you.

A photo you check and save is done. Receipts that arrive by email, from a connected mailbox or in a batch were read automatically, so they wait for a quick look under Review needed:

In the listWhat to do
Missing amount…Open it, compare with the Original tab, fill in what's in red, press Complete receipt.
To confirmEverything was read. Check it against the original and press Confirm receipt.
Nothing shownDone. Your accountant has it.

To fix a mistake later, open the receipt and press Edit receipt. Your accountant sees what was changed.

The Monday email

On Mondays, your accounting firm sends you one email listing what's missing: payments on your bank statement with no receipt yet, and receipts waiting for you. When everything is in order, you get nothing. Reply to it and your accountant gets your answer.

Your accountant may also email you the list of bank payments without a receipt at month end. Add those receipts the usual way; they're matched automatically.

Don't want the Monday email? Settings → Your accountant → switch off Weekly reminder.

Foreign currency?

A receipt in euros or dollars keeps its own amount, and UnReceipt adds the value in kronor at the ECB rate of that day. If your bank charged a different amount, type it in Charged in SEK when you edit the receipt.

Your data, your choices

What your accountant sees, where it's kept, and how to take it with you.

ReceiptYour accountant sees it
Photo or uploadAs soon as you save it.
Forwarded emailAs soon as it arrives.
From your connected Gmail / OutlookOnly after you confirm it. These arrive by themselves, so you get to look first.

Where your data is

Stored in the EU (Frankfurt) and encrypted. UnReceipt never asks for your bank login.

Take your receipts with you

In Settings, Export for your accountant downloads a month or a year as a ZIP: a spreadsheet of every receipt, a PDF of each one and the original files.

Stop sharing or leave

Settings → Your accountant → Stop sharing ends your firm's access at once.

Deleting your account (at the bottom of Settings) first asks you to download your receipts. Swedish bookkeeping law requires your company to keep its receipts for seven years.

Paper receipts

Ask your accountant before you throw paper originals away. Whether a photo is enough depends on the rules that apply to your company, and they will know.
